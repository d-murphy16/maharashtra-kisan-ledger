      Section IV

      What farmers are actually asking about

      Every call's QueryType field, grouped into eight concern buckets. Weather advisory dominates everywhere — the second-order signal is in how the remaining share splits between agronomic help (pest/nutrient) and policy-facing help (schemes/insurance/credit).

      Overall mix · all 5,167,913 Maharashtra calls

      💧
      27,899 calls (0.54%) are explicitly tagged Water Management / Irrigation — a strikingly small share given how drought-prone Marathwada and Vidarbha are. Most water-scarcity concern almost certainly surfaces indirectly, folded into the 1.90M Weather & Climate Advisory calls (36.8% of all queries) rather than logged as an irrigation query. Treat "Weather" volume as an upper bound on water-related concern, not "Water Management" alone.
